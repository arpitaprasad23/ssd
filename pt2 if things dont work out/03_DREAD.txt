EXP 3 - DREAD RISK SCORING

AIM: Score identified threats and prioritize them.

D = Damage potential
R = Reproducibility
E = Exploitability
A = Affected users
D = Discoverability

Use a 1-10 score for each (follow your faculty's scale if different).
Risk score = (Damage + Reproducibility + Exploitability + Affected Users + Discoverability) / 5

Example:
Threat: SQL Injection
Damage 9
Reproducibility 8
Exploitability 8
Affected users 9
Discoverability 7
Total = 41; Average = 8.2 -> HIGH

Threat: weak logout/session handling
7 + 6 + 6 + 6 + 5 = 30; Average = 6.0 -> MEDIUM

Suggested classification for demo: 1-3 Low, >3-6 Medium, >6-10 High. State that thresholds are your lab convention.

STEPS:
1. Take threats from STRIDE.
2. Give each five scores.
3. Calculate average.
4. Sort highest to lowest.
5. Mitigate highest risks first.

RESULT: DREAD scores were calculated and threats prioritized.

VIVA: STRIDE identifies threat TYPES; DREAD prioritizes/rates risk.
